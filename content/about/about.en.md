## About atfedi.de

atfedi.de is a place to look together. It's like a book you can come back to any time.

Here is what we try to keep in mind.

1. Keep it a gentle place.
2. Welcome you, any time.
3. Write what's certain, and keep our own views separate.
4. Listen to what people say, and stay soft.
5. Say thank you.

If you like, come along with us.

You can talk to me, and you can send support too.
