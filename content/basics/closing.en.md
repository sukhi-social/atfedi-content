## When your server is closing

While your server is still running, you can move to another one and take your followers with you. Once it's fully shut down, your followers can't be moved and your posts are hard to get back. So export first, then move.

[icon:footprints] Basics · [icon:server] Mastodon, Misskey, and more · [icon:clock] Do this before it closes

### Know this first

- [icon:triangle-alert] **After the server closes, your followers can't be moved.** A move works because your old server tells everyone "this account has moved over there." A closed server can't send that.
- [icon:triangle-alert] **Export files can only be downloaded while the server is up.** Unless the admins hand out backups themselves, there's no way to get them afterwards.
- [icon:clock] On Mastodon, you can request a post archive only once every 7 days, and it takes a while to build. Request it first.

How much time you have depends on the server. Servers listed on joinmastodon.org promise at least 3 months' notice before shutting down. Others may close with little warning.

### Check that it's really closing

There's no need to rush on a rumor. Look for an announcement from the admins. It's usually in the server's announcements, a post from an admin account, or the server's about page (`/about`). If it gives a closing date and says what you can do until then, plan around that date.

### Before it closes

1. Request your post archive. On Mastodon it's under Preferences › Import and export › Data export. Download it once it's ready.
2. Download the CSV files. The same page has your follows, lists, blocks, mutes, and blocked domains. You'll import them on your new account.
3. Pick a new server and make an account. If a server you chose in a hurry doesn't suit you, you can move again after 30 days.
4. Move. The steps are in [Moving servers](/basics/moving). Add an alias on the new account first, then start the move from the old one.
5. Tell people your new address. Before you finish the move, post something like "Moving here: @name@newserver" from your old account and pin it. Until the server closes, people who missed the move notice can still find you.

Misskey works the same way: export from settings, add the old account as an alias on the new one, then start the move from the old account. Menu names vary a little by server and version.

### If it's already gone

Your followers can't be moved, but you don't have to start from nothing.

- [icon:inbox] If you have a CSV from before, import it on your new account to follow everyone again in one go.
- [icon:search] Without a CSV, search for the people you remember and follow them again. They'll get a notification, and many will follow you back.
- [icon:megaphone] In your first post on the new account, say who you were and which server you were on. People will recognize you.

### For next time

Even if nothing is closing, downloading your CSVs now and then makes a moment like this much calmer. Once a month is plenty.

### Questions

The admins of your server know best about the closing date and backups. If you're not sure who to ask, use the contact at the bottom of atfedi.de.
