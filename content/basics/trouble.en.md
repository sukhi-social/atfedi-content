## When something's wrong

If someone is making you uncomfortable, you can mute or block them right now. They won't be notified. If the same person keeps harassing you, take screenshots before you block them, then report them.

### First

- [icon:camera] If the harassment keeps coming, screenshot the posts and save their links before you block. Once you block someone, their posts disappear from your view, which makes them harder to find when you report.
- [icon:flag] You can report and block at the same time. Reports go to the moderators of your own server.

### Mute, block, report

All three are in the menu (⋯) on a post or a profile. None of them notifies the other person.

- [icon:volume-x] Mute: their posts stop showing up in your timelines. They can still follow you and see your posts. You can mute for a set time, too.
- [icon:ban] Block: if they followed you, that follow is removed, and their replies and mentions no longer reach you. Your public posts can still be read without logging in, so a block doesn't hide them completely.
- [icon:flag] Report: your server's moderators read it and decide what to do. If the person is on another server, you can choose to forward the report to that server's moderators as well. Forwarded reports don't include your name.

When you report, attach the posts in question. A short note on what was wrong helps the moderators decide.

### Avoiding a whole server

If several accounts from one server keep showing up, you can block that entire server (a domain block). You'll stop seeing all posts and notifications from it.

- [icon:triangle-alert] **A domain block removes every follower you have on that server. Unblocking doesn't bring them back.**

### Narrowing notifications and who sees your posts

These help before anything goes wrong, and when you're just tired. Names are from Mastodon and vary a little from server to server.

- [icon:bell-off] Notification filters: put notifications from people you don't follow, or from brand-new accounts, in a separate place. Useful when strangers pile into your mentions.
- [icon:lock] Follow requests: only people you approve become followers. Followers-only posts are seen only by them.
- [icon:eye-off] Post visibility: choose public, quiet public, followers only, or mentioned people only for each post. Mentioned-only posts (DMs) aren't encrypted, so your server's admins can read them.
- [icon:filter] Keyword filters: hide posts containing words you don't want to see.
- [icon:search] Search and discovery: there are settings to keep your posts out of search and to hide who you follow.

### When your server won't help

If your reports go unanswered, or the server's rules don't fit you, it's fine to move to another server. Your followers come with you. The steps are in [Moving servers](/basics/moving).

A server's rules and moderators are usually listed on its about page (`/about`).

### If you want to ask more

Your own server's moderators are usually the quickest people to ask. If you're not sure who to ask, write to the contacts at the bottom of atfedi.de. Small things are welcome too.
